A proposal about presidential pardons

A constitutional amendment idea

The proposal

All presidential pardons going forward are valid for only 90 days unless one-third of either chamber of Congress votes to make them permanent.

How an amendment gets adopted

  • Proposed: Two-thirds of the House of Representatives and two-thirds of the Senate must vote for it.
  • Ratified: The legislatures of three-fourths of the states—38 out of 50—must approve it.

Why I think it could help

The pardon power can be used to show mercy to someone mistreated by the law. This idea keeps that ability, but asks at least some elected representatives to agree before a pardon becomes permanent.

I think both parties have been burned by misuse by their own party AND by the other party. That could make this idea politically popular.

Proposed effective date

November 3, 2026—the day I thought of it, to preempt any pardons trying to front-run ratification.

This is a proposal, not current law. How it could apply to pardons issued before ratification would need to be worked out.
